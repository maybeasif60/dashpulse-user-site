MeetRoom

MeetRoom is a comprehensive productivity and management application built to help users efficiently organize rooms, schedules, and daily workflows.

Why MeetRoom Requests Google Access:

  • Gmail Access: To view and manage user email messages and settings within the workflow.
  • Google Photos: To view the Google Photos library for media integration.
  • Google Drive: To access files required for document management.

You can securely log in using your Google account to access your personalized workspace dashboard.
